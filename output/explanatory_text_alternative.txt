Line chart of the percentage of patients meeting the weekly goal of 150 minutes of exercise (y-axis, %) at four follow-up visits (x-axis, visits 1 to 4) for the Home-based and Center-based rehab programs. Both rise across the visits: Home-based from 58% to 70% and Center-based from 57% to 79%, so the programs start about equal and Center-based finishes 9 points higher at visit 4, 79% against 70%. Patients chose their program, so the gap does not show that center-based rehab causes more exercise, and each percentage covers only the patients seen at that visit, about 45 per program, so a 9-point gap could also come from chance or from who returned.
